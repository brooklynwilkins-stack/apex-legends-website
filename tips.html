GAMEPLAY TIPS

Simple habits that can help new players build confidence.

START STRONG

Five tips for new players

01

Learn one Legend at a time

Practice one character until you understand their abilities and when they are most useful.

02

Stay close to your squad

Share information with pings or voice chat, and try not to take fights alone. This is a team based game.

03

Keep moving and use cover

Use walls, rocks, and buildings to reduce exposure when you need to heal or reload.

04

Manage your gear

Carry a sensible mix of ammunition, healing items, and equipment for your playstyle.

05

Pay attention to the ring

Check the map and plan your route early so your squad has time to rotate safely.
