GAME MODES

Ways to enter the fight

Battle Royale

Squads land on a large map, search for gear, and try to be the last team standing while the playable area gets smaller.

  • Explore and loot carefully.
  • Pay attention to the ring.
  • Coordinate with your squad.

Mixtape

A team-focused mode built around quick engagements and practicing combat in a more direct format when available.

  • Practice aiming and movement.
  • Stick near teammates.
  • Learn how different weapons feel.

Wildcard

Play big, play loud in Wildcard, the new permanent mode built for more shooting, less looting.

  • Faster gameplay compared to battle royale
  • Streamlined upgrades
  • A chance of comebacks.

Game modes and their availability may change over time. Check the official Apex Legends website for current details.
